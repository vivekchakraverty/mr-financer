## New in 0.3.0

**Ideation → Could this idea pay?** helps founders assess an idea using their sales and cost inputs alongside fetched research.

- **Calculate financial scenarios locally.** Compare cautious, base and optimistic scenarios for operating profit after owner's pay, margins, break-even sales, profit targets, startup funding gaps, and simple runway and payback indicators. Mark inputs as actual, estimated, unknown or not applicable; missing inputs stay visible.
- **Create a brief grounded in retrieved sources.** The app selects relevant fetched listings, company research, prices, reviews and financial records, combines them with sourced business guidance and calculated figures, and links the brief's citations to its source records.
- **See geographic and evidence limits.** Each brief identifies the selected market, labels evidence from other or unspecified locations, and distinguishes missing retrieved evidence from information that might exist elsewhere. Listings and founder assumptions are not treated as proof of customer demand.
- **Use ZeroGPU by default, with CPU available.** Qwen3.5-9B generates briefs through the existing Hugging Face model service. Enter your personal Hugging Face token; requests use your account's ZeroGPU quota and eligible credits. Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 remains available as the CPU option, which can take several minutes.
- **Keep calculations authoritative.** The app supplies the financial interpretation from its own calculations and uses sourced pilot guidance to avoid invented numerical targets. Changing inputs or fetched research clears stale results.

The model service now provides both business-plan drafting and the new viability brief through a shared model and queue. Financial previews run locally without model access. These scenarios depend on the inputs supplied; they are not forecasts or guarantees of profit.
